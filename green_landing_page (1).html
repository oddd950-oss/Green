Straight answers to honest questions

The two main reasons people hesitate before joining Green.

1. "Is $250 a month too much for a garden plot?"

Renting raw land yourself requires municipal permits, setting up water taps, buying hundreds of pounds of soil, and buying tools. With Green, $250 pays for the plot, water usage, prepped organic dirt, tools, and ongoing heavy maintenance (like weeding borders and repairing fences). You are paying for a complete ready-to-use setup without the manual labor of maintaining land.

2. "What if my plot isn't ready when I pay or I don't know how to start?"

First, our 14-day guarantee means if your plot isn't ready to plant on day 14, you don't pay a cent until it is. Second, we hand you a prepped bed with healthy soil and automatic irrigation already running. You don't need farming experience—just bring your seeds, poke them into the soil, and water them.
